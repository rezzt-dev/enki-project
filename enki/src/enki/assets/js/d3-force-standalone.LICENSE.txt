This file concatenates the four standalone UMD builds above (d3-force depends on the
other three at runtime; they are not bundled together upstream). Each attaches its
exports onto a shared global `d3` object, in dependency order, so d3-force finds
d3.quadtree/d3.dispatch/d3.timer already defined when it runs as a plain inlined
<script> with no module loader.
